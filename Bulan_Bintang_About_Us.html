About Us

Our Journey

Founded in 2016, Bulan Bintang started with a vision to bring the beauty of the cosmos into everyday life. From our humble beginnings, we've grown into a leading provider of celestial-themed products.

  • 2016: Company founded.
  • 2017: Launched first collection.
  • 2019: Expanded product line.
  • 2023: Opened new flagship store.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To inspire and connect people with the beauty of the cosmos through our unique products.

Vision: To be the leading brand in celestial-themed products, known for quality and innovation.
